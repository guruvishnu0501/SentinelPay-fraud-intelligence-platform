Why Transaction Fraud Detection Matters

Modern financial systems must balance transaction speed, customer convenience and fraud prevention.

The Challenge

Digital payment ecosystems process millions of transactions per second. Fraudsters continually evolve sophisticated attack vectors to exploit payment networks, targeting cardholders through:

Stolen Credentials
Unauthorized use of compromised card details across e-commerce channels.
Account Takeover
Exploiting hijacked user accounts to execute rapid high-value transactions.
Foreign IP Mismatch
Initiating domestic card purchases from overseas IP addresses or proxy networks.
Unusual Velocity
Executing multiple rapid transactions in minutes before cardholders notice.
Why Traditional Rules Are Not Enough

Legacy fraud detection relies on rigid, static rules (such as "Block every transaction above ₹50,000"). Static rules generate severe customer friction and high false positive rates by blocking legitimate customers making genuine high-value purchases. Modern fintech platforms require multi-dimensional risk evaluation where transaction amounts, geographic signals, timing, and behavioral context are analyzed holistically.

Three-Level Risk Response
LOW RISK (Score 0 – 39.99)
ALLOW
Pass transaction seamlessly under standard authorization controls.
MEDIUM RISK (Score 40 – 69.99)
STEP-UP AUTHENTICATION
Request 2FA / OTP verification before completing authorization.
HIGH RISK (Score 70 – 100)
BLOCK
Decline transaction immediately and flag for manual fraud review.
